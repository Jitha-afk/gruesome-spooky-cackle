## Method and access limits

We convert source timestamps to IST and exclude items outside the window. We verify vendor and media handles before account searches. X searches cover vendor posts, media posts and security keywords. We page the main searches and add targeted searches. This is a sample, not a complete archive. We review every Cloudflare Radar Zero Wire RSS item. We rank themes by distinct account and channel names first, then X engagement. Coverage from both vendors and media breaks remaining ties. X engagement adds likes, reposts, replies and quotes. YouTube reach uses watch-page view counts, not search-result estimates. We leave out product launches and mixed breach lists as themes. We list them under also observed. We keep each item in one theme. Some themes group separate incidents by a shared operational concern. We do not add independent posts with fewer than 30 X interactions. We read replies and quotes on the two or three highest-engagement X posts in each theme. We sample one page per endpoint, with up to 25 results. We exclude out-of-window comments, reposts, empty text, unrelated text, abuse and product promotion. We use reviewed keyword rules for English clusters and sentiment. We leave other languages unclassified. Percentages describe the retained sample, not all readers. Samples use exact source fragments and omit usernames. Risk levels and actions come only from cited sources. Actions with the basis type generic are safe general steps, not vendor instructions. We preserve retrieved titles and post text. We do not independently verify every linked article. A source claim remains that source's claim.
