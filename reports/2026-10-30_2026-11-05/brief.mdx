## Method and access limits

We convert source timestamps to IST and exclude items outside the window. We verify vendor and media handles before account searches. X searches cover vendor posts, media posts and security keywords. We page the main vendor and media searches, then add targeted searches. This is a sample, not a complete archive. We count distinct account and channel names first, then X engagement. Coverage from both vendors and media breaks remaining ties. X engagement adds likes, reposts, replies and quotes. YouTube reach uses watch-page view counts, not search-result estimates. We keep each item in one theme. Some themes group separate incidents by a shared operational concern. We do not add independent posts with fewer than 30 X interactions. We read replies and quotes on the two highest-engagement X posts in each theme. We sample one page per endpoint, with up to 25 results. We exclude out-of-window comments, reposts, empty text, unrelated text, abuse and repeated product promotion. We use reviewed keyword rules for English clusters and sentiment. We leave other languages unclassified. Percentages describe the retained sample, not all readers. Samples use exact source fragments and omit usernames. We preserve retrieved titles and post text. We do not independently verify every linked article. A source claim remains that source's claim.
